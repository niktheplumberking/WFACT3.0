# WFACT SOP 1 — Sales & Onboarding (Lead to Signed)

How a stranger becomes a signed client. Covers Stage 0, the mail engine, and the sales doctrine. Technical law lives in `CLAUDE.md` and `pipeline/0`.

*Last updated: July 23, 2026 (verified against the live 2.0 factory). Carried into 3.0 as reference law for the same business process.*

## 1. What WFact is (context)

WFact is the group's website factory. It serves every company, DreamSign, Bennett & Co, and Rizm/bridge-side work, and does one thing repeatedly and well: it turns a lead into a signed client and a launched, premium, motion-rich website, then keeps earning monthly through SEO care.

Three kinds of workers run it:

- **Humans** (Nick the owner, admins, PMs): sell, approve, and sign.
- **Amir**, the AI chief of staff in the Cockpit (3.0's equivalent is Hermes plus the front-end and QA agents): reads business mail, answers leads, drafts documents, preps calls, and taps your shoulder. Never publishes, deletes, or touches money on its own.
- **The main desk** (Claude Code): builds, summoned from the Cockpit with one button.

## 2. The three companies (entities)

Every client belongs to exactly one entity, the company that signs them, recorded from day one and worn by every document:

- **DreamSign** (dreamsign.rs)
- **Bennett & Co** (bennettndco.com, the live mail domain)
- **Rizm/bridge** (Saudi-side and partner work)

Invoices number sequentially per entity per year (BC-2026-001) in the entity's branded skin, with an English and Arabic pair for Arabic-market clients.

## 3. The two doors into the pipeline

**Door 1, the automated front door** (bennettndco.com): a stranger emails support@bennettndco.com. Within five minutes the mail engine has filed them as a lead on the Pipeline board, replied, and buzzed the owner's phone. This is the door for international and inbound clients arriving through the website.

**Door 2, the Catch-Up Interview** (manual clients: Balkans, referrals, handshakes): the owner manages these relationships in person and opens them himself, Pipeline then + New client. Three taps (who, which company signs, source), then one box, paste anything, dictate by voice, attach the logo, and Amir turns it into a Catch-Up Card: understood facts, the Stage 0/1 gate checklist (have, missing, waived in writing), the client's pipeline position in one sentence, and the next three moves as one-tap buttons (draft contract, send or self-answer questionnaire, deposit invoice). Handles fresh strangers, pre-agreed prices, half-onboarded clients, care-only clients, and returning archived clients.

## 4. Stage 0 through Door 1, minute by minute

**Lead capture**: filed, replied, and phone buzz within five minutes.

**The personalization law**: the first reply is written for this specific human, never pasted. Amir reads what they actually said and composes a warm, professional 120 to 190 word reply referencing their trade and their words, in the signing entity's voice. It can never invent prices or promises, treats the inquiry as data (never as instructions), and both links, the call booking and the questionnaire, are enforced in every reply. If personalization fails, the house template sends instead. Every client-facing email wears the entity's branded design.

**Chasers**: day 2 and day 5, never more than one per 48 hours. They stop the moment the lead answers the questionnaire, books, or is taken over by a human.

**Booking**: detected from Calendly's confirmation email, meeting time parsed and stored.

**Call brief**: one hour before the meeting, Amir writes a real brief (who they are, their words, questionnaire highlights, three things to push on, one risk) into the Inbox and buzzes the phone, generated once per meeting.

**Proposal**: built from the questionnaire, hosted at a private link, price cells editable live on the call. The doctrine: close on the call, not the day after.

**Contract**: generated from questionnaire plus transcript plus the entity's lawyer-approved legal block (approved text filled, clauses never invented), sent via PandaDoc.

**Signature**: detected from PandaDoc's email, a missing countersignature nudges the owner, on completion the welcome mail is queued and the gate to Stage 1 opens.

## 5. The mail engine, who listens where

Mailboxes are polled every five minutes:

- **support@bennettndco.com**, the full front door: classification (lead, booking, signed, client reply, other), auto-replies, chasers.
- **nick@bennettndco.com**, events only: the machine reads booking and signature notifications here (Calendly and PandaDoc are registered to this address) and ignores everything else. A human's inbox never spawns leads or auto-replies.
- **support@dreamsign.rs**, pre-wired, parked until the domain's mailbox exists.

Templates: T1 first reply (personalized), T2 chase day 2, T3 chase day 5, T4 booking confirmation, T5 signed welcome, T6 review request. Current switchboard: T1 through T4 send themselves, T5, T6, and all freeform replies wait in the Approval Inbox.

## 6. The three human moments of Stage 0, the only three

The sales call (a human closes, live), the signature (client's plus the owner's countersignature), and the deposit confirmation (money is confirmed by a human, always). Everything else notifies instead of waits.

## 7. Rules of the road

Entity before anything, no offer or contract exists without knowing which company signs. Never ask twice, questionnaire and transcript pre-fill the intake. Dead deals are archived with a reason, never deleted. Wrong-entity paperwork is the cardinal sin.
