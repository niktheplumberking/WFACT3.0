# WFACT SOP 2 — Production Pipeline (Stages 1–8)

How a signed client becomes a launched website. The deeper law lives in `CLAUDE.md` (the thirteen directives) and `pipeline/1–8`.

*Last updated: July 23, 2026 (verified against the live 2.0 factory). Carried into 3.0 as reference law for the same business process.*

## 1. The gate principle

The pipeline has eleven stages (0 to 10). A project moves only through a gate check, an honest pass or fail list verified against reality. Nothing ever "slides through." The summonable main desk can carry any stage headlessly, but it can never pass a human gate by itself, it logs the blocker and stops. Cockpit approval taps are the gate evidence.

## 2. Stage 1 — Intake (pre-filled)

Starts pre-filled from the questionnaire and transcript, the client is never asked the same question twice. Missing items (logo, domain access, photography, who edits after launch) get chased the day they are first mentioned. Arabic-market clients: the country is recorded here, Saudi, UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman differ in currency decimals, VAT, and weekends. Gate: everything present or waived in writing.

## 3. Stage 2 — Research, Story & the Total Spec

The intake pile becomes three documents everything downstream obeys: brand DNA (exact colors, fonts, voice, motion personality), story map (every page, section by section: message, motion, assets, self-editable zones, SEO fields), and the Total Spec, the film script of the site: every layer, value, easing, and asset brief decided before any code.

**The Concept Pitch**: before the full spec, the Director presents 2 to 3 named concepts, the lead one marked "(Recommended)", each with a narrated scroll walkthrough, its experience mode, chapter count, and credit estimate. The owner or client picks or blends.

**Experience mode** (in every spec): standard-cinematic (the classic build), scroll-film-A (the whole page is one continuous shot in pure code, no asset cost), scroll-film-B (chained generated footage scrubbed on canvas, the flagship "Film Site" look). Lane choice is a Director plus Cost-Sentinel decision, never a client burden. Human moment: the owner approves the direction (brand DNA plus Total Spec plus styleframes).

## 4. Stage 3 — Assets

Every asset slot filled: brand-matched images and video generated in-house (Higgsfield, palette-locked), compressed, and self-hosted, zero third-party hotlinks ever. Protagonist footage first. Credits are quoted and approved before the first draft is generated.

## 5. Stage 4 — Homepage build

The homepage is built completely, real copy, real assets, full motion, exactly to the spec. Deviation is a bug, not creativity. Before any human sees a link, the build is scroll-recorded and reviewed frame by frame against the spec and the Experience Charter, eyes before humans, including the jank test (worst frame under 50ms, never judged on averages). Human moment: the owner's internal approval.

## 6. Stage 5 — Direction lock

The client sees the homepage. Human moments: the client's written approval, and the milestone payment (invoice drafted by Amir, the owner's approve mints the payment link and emails it). The point of no return on direction, later changes are scope changes, quoted separately.

## 7. Stage 6 — Full build + the Owner's Key

Every remaining page, built to the locked spec, one section at a time, each committed as it finishes. The Owner's Key is wired here: the client's own login edits exactly the texts, images, and prices marked editable, nothing else, the design cannot be broken from the client's side. E-commerce: story pages get the full cinematic treatment, shop pages (listings, cart, checkout) are conversion-first by law.

## 8. Stage 7 — QA + the security pass

A written report where every line is verified against reality: performance on a throttled connection, motion on real phones, every form actually submitted, the Owner's Key round trip, the isolation attack test (one client's login can never touch another's content), admin surfaces unreachable by normal accounts at both app and database layers, all working credentials rotated, no secrets in the repo, and the SEO audit score. Human moment: the owner's ten-minute real-device script, done personally on his phone.

## 9. Stage 8 — Launch & delivery

Production ships via the delivery robot to Hostinger or the client's own host, never Vercel (previews and the internal Cockpit only). The robot uploads the finished site only, never raw clips or build intermediates. The human ship checklist covers the roughly 10 minutes only hands can do: create the site in Hostinger, paste deploy secrets, point the domain (A-record only). The client receives the Closing Report: what was built, real scores, the Owner's Key manual, the care plan offer, the review link, and the portfolio permission ask. Human moments: the launch click, and the final payment confirmation.

## 10. What the machine enforces throughout

Spec before code. Commit and push after every completed section. Real content only, missing content chased never faked. The Experience Charter outranks taste debates (living canvas, scroll-owned motion, one coherent world, any frame a poster). A blocked gate is information, not an obstacle, surface it, never work around it.
