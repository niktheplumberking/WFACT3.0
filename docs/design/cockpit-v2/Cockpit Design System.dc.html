The concept: an editorial control room

{{ p.t }}

{{ p.d }}

Simple and Detailed

One product, one set of screens. Detail is a per-person setting, switchable from the bottom of the menu, the More tab on phones, the command palette or the D key. Role decides what you may do; detail level decides how much you see.

Simple

Default for owners and PMs. Plain sentences, durations rounded, every Technical details drawer closed. Nothing is hidden that changes a decision: money, missing details and placeholders always show.

Detailed

Default for Huraira and admins. Technical details open, ids and exact timings visible, copy buttons on every id, model names and tokens in drawers, check slugs next to their plain names.

Colour

Warm paper neutrals carry everything. Three status colours mean one thing each and always come with a glyph and a word: a diamond for needs you, a tick for done, a stop sign for stopped. Running is ink with a turning ring; waiting is a hollow circle. The accent is a personal choice for buttons and focus only; it never means status.

Type

Newsreader is the display face: an editorial serif with real character at large sizes and a warm italic for the client's own words. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, kept from today's Cockpit, is the reading face, designed for low-vision readers. Overpass Mono, also kept, is for figures, times and ids, with tabular numbers.

Motion

  • Enter: 180 to 400 ms, ease, 6 px rise. Only for things that newly arrived.
  • Running ring: 1.1 s turn. Progress bar: 800 ms ease on change.
  • Nothing loops for decoration. Reduce motion (system or setting) stops every animation; states still read from words and glyphs.

Buttons

Verbs on every button. A button that spends money always opens a confirmation that says roughly how much; anything undoable shows Undo or Changed my mind in its toast.

Everywhere

  • Skip link to main; on navigation, focus moves to the h1 and the document title updates.
  • Focus order: menu, top bar (search, notifications, New request), page content top to bottom, sticky action bar last. Dialogs trap focus and return it to the button that opened them.
  • Every icon is aria-hidden; every icon-only button has a label. Pressed filters use aria-pressed, the view switch and kind of site use radiogroup.
  • No horizontal scroll at 375 px: wide tables scroll inside their own region; everything else wraps.
  • Text size setting scales the whole page; layouts are fluid so 200% zoom holds.
  • There is no deploy or publish control anywhere. “Record that it is live” only records a date after a person launched it.
