How we approach a roof

We treat every roof like it's the one over our own kitchen. That means we climb up, look closely, and tell you exactly what we see, good or bad.

We don't push a bigger job than you need. If a repair will hold, we say so. If it won't, we explain why and what the alternative looks like.

No stock photos here
